# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026 (version 3.0.0)_

Aerie runs on your computer. It goes online only for features you turn on or use yourself: Discover (free pictures, skies and 3D models), the AI wallpaper maker if you pick Claude or ChatGPT, and web wallpapers or web sites you add.

- **We collect nothing.** Aerie has no accounts, sign-ups, analytics, telemetry, crash reports, ads or tracking. We (the publisher) never receive anything from your computer.
- **Offline by default.** Aerie's screens are pages served by its own small server, which listens only on `127.0.0.1` (your own computer) and needs a random key that changes every time Aerie starts. Pop!_OS's updater or the COSMIC Store downloads updates; Aerie doesn't.
- **Discover (off until you turn it on):** Studio → Discover asks first, and Settings → Online content turns it off again.
  - **Pictures** come from [Openverse](https://openverse.org) (`api.openverse.org`), run by the WordPress Foundation. Your search words go to Openverse. A picture you add downloads from the site that hosts it, often Flickr or Wikimedia Commons, which sees an ordinary download. Aerie only offers public-domain and CC0 pictures and saves the credit with each one. [Openverse's privacy policy](https://openverse.org/privacy) applies.
  - **360° skies and 3D models** come from Poly Haven (`api.polyhaven.com`, `cdn.polyhaven.com`, `dl.polyhaven.org`). Aerie downloads the catalog and searches it on your computer, then downloads only the previews and files you pick. [Poly Haven's privacy policy](https://polyhaven.com/privacy) applies.
  - Each of these sites sees your internet address and that the request comes from Aerie (its version). Nothing else about you, your files or your setup is sent. What you downloaded stays in `~/.local/share/aerie/` and keeps working offline.
- **The AI wallpaper maker (off until you set it up):** you pick which AI it uses.
  - **Ollama** runs on your own computer. Nothing leaves it.
  - **Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI):** when you ask for a wallpaper, Aerie sends your request to that company with your own API key. To find or build a wallpaper it also sends the names and descriptions of the matching Openverse pictures or Poly Haven assets so the AI can pick one. When you ask it to change a shader or 3D scene, it sends that wallpaper's code or layout only if the box "Send this … to the AI with my request" is ticked; the box shows under your request. **Paint** (ChatGPT only) sends your request to OpenAI's image service and saves the picture it returns. Aerie never sends your files, notes, widgets, app list or anything else about your computer. That company's terms and privacy policy apply ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)), and it may charge your account.
  - **Your API keys** sit only in `~/.config/aerie/assistant.json`, readable only by your user account, and go only to the service they belong to. "Forget" removes a key. Aerie doesn't keep your requests after the wallpaper is made.
- **Music-reactive wallpapers:** when a wallpaper reacts to sound and something is playing, Aerie listens to **what your computer plays through its speakers** (the "monitor" of your sound output), never your microphone. It turns the sound into 128 loudness levels on the fly and passes them to the wallpaper. It records nothing, stores nothing and sends nothing. Settings → **Let wallpapers react to music** turns it off.
- **What is stored, and where:** your setup (including text you type in the Notes widget), wallpapers you add, make or download, and their credits, in `~/.config/aerie/` and `~/.local/share/aerie/`, plus a small log in `~/.cache/aerie.log`. If you turn on "Start when I log in", Aerie adds one file: `~/.config/autostart/io.github.jayswansocool48.Aerie.desktop`.
- **Your wallpapers:** files you add are copied to `~/.local/share/aerie/wallpapers/`. Aerie looks in Steam's folders for Wallpaper Engine wallpapers you subscribed to. It reads their files, never changes them and never contacts Steam. Wallpaper Engine scenes are unpacked once into `~/.local/share/aerie/cache/`.
- **Web wallpapers and web sites:** these are other people's code. They run in a sandbox with a private, throw-away browser profile and can't see Aerie's settings, your files, other sites' cookies, your camera, microphone or location. A web site wallpaper loads live from the internet, so that site sees an ordinary visit and its own privacy policy applies. Some web wallpapers load things from the internet themselves.
- **Your windows:** to pause while you use apps, Aerie reads which windows are maximized, full screen, focused or minimized, and on which screen and workspace. It doesn't read window titles or contents, and stores none of this.
- **What it reads, without storing it:** the list of installed apps and their icons (for the app drawer and widgets); CPU, memory, disk, battery and network speed (for the System widgets); and what your music or video player is playing (for the Now playing widget and the lock screen). Aerie shows these on your screen and nowhere else.
- **Lock screen (off until you turn it on):** Aerie hands your password to your system's PAM (the same check as logging in) and never stores, logs or sends it. The lock screen shows your name and profile picture from your account, what's playing and your battery level. Turning it on makes one system change you confirm with your password (see the README). Turning it off, removing the switch or uninstalling undoes it.
- **Cookies:** none, so there is no cookie banner. Studio keeps one convenience in its local storage on your computer: the last wallpaper list, so the library shows at once. It never leaves your computer and holds no key or password.
- **Children:** Aerie collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Claude and ChatGPT need an account with those companies, which set their own age rules; children should use Ollama or no AI. Discover never shows pictures Openverse marks as mature, and Studio hides Wallpaper Engine wallpapers rated for adults unless you turn them on in Settings.
- **Deleting your data:** Studio → Settings → **Delete all my Aerie data** removes your setup, wallpapers you added or made, downloads, saved API keys, your lock screen choice, the autostart entry and the shortcuts older Aerie versions added, and gives COSMIC back any look settings older versions changed. Studio → Settings → **Uninstall Aerie** removes the app and all of that data in one step, and takes the lock screen switch away (and the update source, if one was added).
- **Moved from Deskscape:** on first start Aerie copies your Deskscape setup and files from `~/.config/deskscape` and `~/.local/share/deskscape` into its own folders and leaves the old folders alone.
- **Links:** Quick links and the search widget's optional web search open in your normal browser, whose own privacy policy applies.
